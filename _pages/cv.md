## Research interests
{: .sec-title}
I am currently doing research in robotics. My goal is to help robots understand what is around them, know where they are, and work out how to act, and whether it is safe to act, when their surroundings change. At PhD level, I would like to go deeper into teams of robots and sensors: when one member quietly sees or performs worse than it appears, adding it can make the whole team worse, and I want to find how a team could learn from evidence how much to trust each member.
