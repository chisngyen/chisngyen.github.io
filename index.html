About Me

Who I am & what I work on

I'm Trần Chí Nguyên (Chi-Nguyen Tran), a passionate undergraduate student at the Faculty of Information Technology, Ho Chi Minh City University of Science (HCMUS), Vietnam National University — Class of K23 AI (2023–2027).

My research covers Vision-Language Models, Cross-View Geo-Localization, and Autonomous Drone Navigation. Right now I'm mostly figuring out how to match drone photos to satellite maps so drones can know where they are without GPS — and on the side, studying how LLMs behave in game-theoretic settings.

I've published at venues including ACM Multimedia, MSR, ALTA, and MMLoSo — all as part of Team HCMUS_TheFangs, a research group I work in with classmates and two professors from the UK.
